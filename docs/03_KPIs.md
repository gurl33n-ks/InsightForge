# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

## Purpose

This document defines the primary Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) that InsightForge will monitor.

These KPIs help NovaMart's leadership measure business performance, identify opportunities, and support strategic decision-making.



# Executive KPIs

## 1. Total Revenue

### Description

The total revenue generated from all completed customer orders.

### Business Value

Measures the overall financial performance of the company.

### Formula

Total Revenue = Sum of all order payments

---

## 2. Total Orders

### Description

The total number of completed customer orders.

### Business Value

Measures customer demand.

---

## 3. Average Order Value (AOV)

### Description

Average amount spent per order.

### Formula

AOV = Total Revenue ÷ Total Orders

### Business Value

Helps determine customer purchasing behavior.



## 4. Monthly Revenue Growth

### Description

Percentage increase or decrease in revenue compared to the previous month.

### Business Value

Tracks company growth over time.


# Customer KPIs

## 5. Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

### Description

Estimated total value generated by a customer throughout their relationship with the company.

### Business Value

Identifies high-value customers.



## 6. Repeat Purchase Rate

### Description

Percentage of customers who placed more than one order.

### Business Value

Measures customer loyalty.



## 7. Customer Satisfaction Score

### Description

Average review score provided by customers.

### Business Value

Measures customer experience.



# Operations KPIs

## 8. Average Delivery Time

### Description

Average number of days between purchase and delivery.

### Business Value

Measures logistics efficiency.



## 9. On-Time Delivery Rate

### Description

Percentage of deliveries completed on or before the estimated delivery date.

### Business Value

Measures shipping performance.



# Seller KPIs

## 10. Seller Performance Score

### Description

Performance metric combining:

- Revenue
- Number of Orders
- Average Review Rating
- Delivery Performance

### Business Value

Identifies top-performing sellers.



# Product KPIs

## 11. Best Selling Products

### Description

Products generating the highest number of sales.

### Business Value

Supports inventory planning.



## 12. Product Category Performance

### Description

Revenue and order volume by product category.

### Business Value

Identifies high-performing and underperforming categories.
