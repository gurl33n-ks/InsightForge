# InsightForge

## Business Requirements Document

---

# Project Overview

InsightForge is an enterprise retail intelligence platform developed for a fictional multinational e-commerce company called NovaMart.

The platform helps executives monitor business performance and make strategic decisions using sales, customer, product, seller, payment, logistics, and review data.

---

# Business Problem

NovaMart has experienced rapid growth.

Leadership struggles to answer important business questions because information is scattered across multiple systems.

Executives need one centralized platform that provides real-time insights into business performance.

---

# Project Goal

Build an end-to-end analytics platform that transforms operational retail data into strategic business insights.

---

# Stakeholders

• Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

• Sales Director

• Marketing Director

• Operations Manager

• Customer Success Manager

• Finance Team

---

# Success Criteria

The platform should enable stakeholders to:

- Monitor sales performance
- Understand customer behavior
- Evaluate seller performance
- Track delivery efficiency
- Analyze customer reviews
- Monitor revenue trends
- Identify business opportunities
